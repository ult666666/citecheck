# Parental Leave

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after 90 days of employment. The policy applies equally to birth, adoption and foster placement, and to parents of any gender.

## Leave length and pay

Eligible employees receive 16 weeks of leave at full pay. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or placement.

## Taking leave in blocks

Leave can be taken all at once or split into up to 3 blocks. Each block must be at least 2 weeks long. When possible, tell your manager and the People team at least 30 days before the first block starts.

## Returning to work

After leave, employees may use a phased return: up to 4 weeks on a reduced schedule of 60 percent of normal hours, at full pay.
