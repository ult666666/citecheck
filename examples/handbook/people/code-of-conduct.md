# Code of Conduct

## Respect at work

Larkmoor expects everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated, whether in the office, online or at company events.

## Reporting concerns

Report concerns to your manager, to the People team, or anonymously through the ethics hotline, which is available 24 hours a day. Reports are investigated promptly and kept as confidential as possible.

## No retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited and is itself a violation of this code.

## Gifts from vendors

You may accept gifts from vendors or customers worth up to $50 per person per year. Anything above that value must be reported to the People team, who decide whether it can be kept.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any potential conflict of interest, such as a side business or a family member who works at a vendor, to your manager within 14 days of becoming aware of it.
