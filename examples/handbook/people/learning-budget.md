# Learning and Development Budget

## Annual budget

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, workshops and conference registration fees. Unused budget does not roll over into the next year.

## Conferences

Conference registration fees come out of the learning budget. Travel and lodging for an approved conference are paid from the team's travel budget instead and follow the travel policy. Request manager approval for a conference at least 30 days before it starts.

## Certifications

Larkmoor pays exam fees for job-related certifications from the learning budget. If you do not pass, the company covers one retake per certification per year.

## Paying and getting reimbursed

Buy approved learning items with your own card and submit the receipt through the expense tool, tagged as learning. Purchases over $500 need written manager approval before you buy.
