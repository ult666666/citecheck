# Onboarding

## Your first day

New employees pick up their laptop from IT at 9:30 a.m. on their first day, followed by an IT setup session. For remote employees, IT ships the laptop so that it arrives at least 2 business days before the start date.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from another team for their first 30 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions; your manager owns your goals.

## Required training

Complete the security awareness training within your first 5 business days. Code of conduct training must be finished within your first 30 days.

## Check-ins

Managers hold structured check-ins with new hires at 30, 60 and 90 days to review goals and answer questions.
