# Performance Reviews

## Review cycles

Larkmoor runs two review cycles each year, in April and October. Employees who joined less than 90 days before a cycle starts skip that cycle.

## Review steps

Each cycle has four steps: a written self-review, feedback from 3 to 5 peers chosen with your manager, a written manager review, and a calibration meeting where managers compare ratings across teams.

## Ratings

Ratings use a four-point scale: Does Not Meet Expectations, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations and Outstanding.

## Promotions

Promotions are decided only in the October cycle. The manager writes the promotion case, and a promotion committee reviews it. The committee always includes at least one person from outside the employee's department.
