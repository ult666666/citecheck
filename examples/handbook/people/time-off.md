# Paid Time Off

Larkmoor Analytics gives every full-time employee paid time off (PTO) for vacation, personal days and anything else they choose. Sick leave is separate and does not use PTO.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, which adds up to 21 days per calendar year. Accrual starts on your first day of work.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal. For absences longer than three consecutive working days, submit the request at least 10 business days in advance. Your manager approves or declines each request within 3 business days.

## Carryover

Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31, and any that remain after that date expire. Unused PTO beyond the 5-day carryover limit is forfeited on December 31 and is not paid out.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 8 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from PTO. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required only for absences of more than 3 consecutive sick days.

## Company holidays

Larkmoor observes 11 company holidays each year. The People team publishes the holiday calendar for the following year every December.
