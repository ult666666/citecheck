# Benefits

## Health insurance

Medical, dental and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date. Larkmoor pays 90 percent of the premium for employees and 75 percent of the premium for covered dependents.

## Open enrollment

Open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15 each year. Outside open enrollment, coverage can change only within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## Retirement plan

Larkmoor matches 100 percent of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4 percent of salary. Company matching contributions vest immediately.

## Wellness stipend

Every employee receives a wellness stipend of $60 per month for gym memberships, fitness classes or mental health apps. Unused stipend does not roll over to the next month.
