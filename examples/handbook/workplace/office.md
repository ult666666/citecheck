# Offices and Facilities

## Locations and hours

Larkmoor has offices in Pittsburgh and Lisbon. Both buildings are open from 7:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. Outside those hours, enter with your badge.

## Desk booking

Desks are shared. Book a desk in the office app up to 2 weeks in advance.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the visitor system at least 24 hours before they arrive. Visitors must sign in at reception and be escorted at all times.

## Lost badges

Report a lost or stolen badge to facilities within 24 hours so that it can be deactivated. The first replacement badge is free; each later replacement costs $20.
