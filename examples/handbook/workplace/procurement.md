# Purchasing and Vendors

## Approval limits

Purchases under $1,000 need approval from your manager. Purchases from $1,000 to $10,000 need approval from your department head and Finance. Purchases over $10,000 need approval from the chief financial officer and at least two competing quotes.

## New software vendors

Every new software vendor must pass a security review before a contract is signed. Plan for the review to take about 2 weeks.

## Contracts

Only Finance and Legal may sign vendor contracts on behalf of Larkmoor. Do not accept click-through terms for paid services on the company's behalf.
