# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Hybrid schedule

Employees who live within commuting distance of an office work there two days a week, on Tuesday and Wednesday. Fully remote roles require approval from a vice president.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be available for meetings and messages during core hours, 11:00 to 15:00 US Eastern Time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

## Home office support

Remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for equipment such as a desk, chair or monitor, plus $40 per month toward home internet.

## Working from another country

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year with approval from the People team. Longer stays need a legal and tax review before they can be approved.
