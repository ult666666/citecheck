# Business Travel

## Booking

Book flights, hotels and rental cars through the travel portal. Book at least 14 days before departure when possible; late bookings cost more and need a short explanation.

## Flights

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is allowed. Business class is never reimbursed.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is $250 in most cities and $325 in high-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, London and Zurich.

## Meals and per diem

For domestic trips, the meal per diem is $75 per day, which covers all meals and tips. The per diem replaces individual meal receipts.

## Rental cars

Rent a compact or midsize car. Larkmoor's corporate insurance covers rental cars, so decline the rental company's collision damage waiver.
