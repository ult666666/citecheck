# Communication Guidelines

## Choosing a channel

Use chat for quick questions and email for anything that involves people outside the company. Decisions that affect more than one team are written down in the team's decision log.

## Response times

During core hours, reply to direct chat messages within 4 working hours. Email from customers gets a first response within 1 business day.

## Focus Thursdays

Thursdays are reserved for focused work: no internal meetings are scheduled on Thursdays, except for incident response.

## Announcements

Only the People team and the leadership team post in the company-wide announcements channel. Everyone else shares news in their team channels.

## Weekly updates

Each team posts a short written status update by noon US Eastern Time every Friday.
