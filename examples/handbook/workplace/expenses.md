# Expense Reimbursement

## Submitting expenses

Submit business expenses in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase date. Expenses submitted later than that need written approval from your department head.

## Receipts

Attach an itemized receipt for every expense of $25 or more. For expenses under $25, a receipt is recommended but not required.

## Approval and payment

Your manager reviews each expense report within 5 business days. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next regular payroll.

## Corporate cards

Employees who travel or buy for their team regularly can request a corporate card. Purchases over $500 must be made on a corporate card or approved in advance by your manager.

## What is not reimbursed

Larkmoor does not reimburse alcohol (except at approved team events), personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, or flight and hotel upgrades that the travel policy does not allow.
