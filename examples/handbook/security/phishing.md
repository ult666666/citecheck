# Reporting Phishing

## How to report

Report suspicious email with the Report Phishing button in the mail client. Do not forward suspicious messages to colleagues.

## If you clicked

If you clicked a link in a suspicious email or entered your password, report it immediately in the security help channel and change your password right away. Reporting quickly matters more than anything else.

## Phishing simulations

The security team runs phishing simulations every quarter. Clicking a simulated phishing link carries no penalty, but you will be asked to complete a short refresher training within 7 days.
