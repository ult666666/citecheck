# Incident Response

## Severity levels

SEV1: a customer-facing outage or a confirmed data exposure. SEV2: a degraded service or a partial outage. SEV3: a minor issue with no customer impact.

## Response times

For a SEV1, the on-call engineer pages the incident commander immediately. A SEV2 must be acknowledged within 30 minutes. SEV3 issues are handled on the next business day.

## Roles

Every SEV1 and SEV2 has an incident commander, who coordinates the response, and a communications lead, who posts status updates. During a SEV1, updates are posted every 30 minutes.

## Postmortems

A blameless postmortem is written for every SEV1 and SEV2 within 5 business days of resolution. Postmortems focus on causes and fixes, not on individuals.
