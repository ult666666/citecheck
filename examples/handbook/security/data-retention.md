# Data Retention

## Customer data

When a customer contract ends, the customer's data is deleted from production systems within 30 days. Backups containing that data are purged within 90 days.

## Logs

Application logs are kept for 13 months. Security audit logs are kept for 3 years.

## Support tickets

Personal data in support tickets is redacted 180 days after the ticket is closed.

## Legal holds

A legal hold overrides every schedule in this policy. Data under a legal hold is kept until Legal releases the hold.
