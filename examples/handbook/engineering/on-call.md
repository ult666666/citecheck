# On-Call

## Rotation

On-call shifts last one week and hand off on Mondays at 10:00 US Eastern Time. Engineers join the rotation after 3 months at Larkmoor and are on call at most one week in four.

## Responding to pages

Acknowledge a page within 5 minutes and be at a computer, working on the problem, within 15 minutes.

## Compensation

Engineers receive an on-call stipend of $400 for each week on call. If you are paged between 22:00 and 07:00, you may start work late or take the next morning off.

## Handoff

At the end of each shift, write a handoff note that lists open issues, recent pages and anything the next engineer should watch.
